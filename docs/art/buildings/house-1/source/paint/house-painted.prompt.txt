Match J's warm grounded stylization but push the brush-painted surface quality further than the input render. Reduce the physically rendered CGI/model appearance, tiny texture noise and overly smooth material shading. Crisp readable silhouette, painterly interior detail, no heavy black outlines. Keep sufficient crafted material definition for HQ, not flat cartoon or blurry watercolor.
QUALITY
The house will be displayed on a 512px square canvas with approximately 96px door opening next to an 88px adult. Prioritize material and shape readability at that size. Preserve geometry rather than increasing detail. No text, watermark, UI, props, vegetation, background color, checkerboard, new windows, chimney or perspective change. Genuine alpha outside the building.
