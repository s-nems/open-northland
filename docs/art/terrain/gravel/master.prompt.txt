Create a new rocky foothill ground material pair for Open Northland. Output purpose: production terrain texture masters.

Left half: fine angular gravel and tiny embedded weathered stones on subdued warm grey-brown earth. Right half: quiet exposed weathered grey bedrock, shallow broad irregular planes and restrained small cracks with a little earth in crevices. Both are flat overhead orthographic material surfaces, fully opaque, edge-to-edge, even detail density, no horizon or perspective.
